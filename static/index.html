Our Guiding Light

Vision (දැක්ම)

“අභියෝග ජයගත හැකි ගුණ නැණ සපිරි ශිෂ්‍යයෙක් සමාජයට දායාද කිරීම ජයකොඩි මාතාවගේ දැක්මයි.”

To mould a generation of disciplined, intellectually empowered, and virtuous citizens who proudly uphold Sri Lankan cultural heritage.

Our Commitment

Mission (මෙහෙවර)

“අධ්‍යාපනයේ මූලික අරමුණ ඉටුකර ගැනීම සඳහා මූලිකත්වය ලබා දෙමින් ජාතික නිපුණතාවලට අනුකූලව ගුණාත්මක අධ්‍යාපන සංවර්ධනය සඳහා කැපවීම ජයකොඩි මාතාවගේ මෙහෙවරයි.”

Dedicated to qualitative educational empowerment aligning with national competencies and moral development.

About Jayakody Maha Vidyalaya

Founded on October 14, 1956 under the sacred blessings of Most Venerable Welivitiye Soratha Nayaka Thero and the generous philanthropy of Mrs. Siridattha Jayakody and Mr. Kamalawarna Jayakody, the school was incorporated into the prestigious Navodya School Development Project in 1966.

Census Number: 11084
Established: October 14, 1956 (70+ Years of Legacy)
Official Colors: Royal Maroon & Imperial Gold
Zone / Division: Kelaniya Educational Zone • Mahara Division
Principal: Mr. P. Senevirathne Banda (Bachelor of Arts)
